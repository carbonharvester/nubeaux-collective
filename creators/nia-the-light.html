About Nia The Light

Nia The Light is a London-born content creator with Zimbabwean roots. She's an international model, published author, entrepreneur, and one of the most recognised voices globally in the natural hair movement. With 120+ brand partnerships over 12 years, Nia has worked with Park Hyatt, Anantara, Onguma Safari Camps, Dyson, Pandora, and Estée Lauder, to name a few, delivering campaigns that resonate with high-value audiences.

At 16, she founded #HappyFroDay, a celebration of natural hair that started in Hyde Park and grew into an international movement. In 2020, she opened The Curl Bar London, a salon dedicated to curly, coily, and wavy hair. In 2021, Hay House published her debut book, The Book of Light: Illuminate Your Life with Self-Love.

What sets Nia apart is the depth of connection with her audience. Her content goes beyond tutorials and sponsored posts. She builds trust through authenticity, vulnerability, and showing up as herself. That philosophy carries through to everything she creates, from luxury travel content to home and lifestyle. Her audience doesn't just watch. They trust her, they engage, and they convert.
