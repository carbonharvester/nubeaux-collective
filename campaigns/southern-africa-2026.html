Southern Africa

January 5 — January 14, 2026

A 10-day luxury content campaign across three Southern African destinations in partnership with Park Hyatt, Four Seasons, Onguma Safari Camps, and Anantara.

Creators

This campaign was designed as a single, coherent narrative rather than a collection of isolated stays, with each destination building on the last. Partner properties were positioned within an integrated story that highlighted atmosphere, service, design and sense of place. Each partner received dedicated in-feed Reels, story coverage throughout the stay, and full usage rights in perpetuity for all agreed assets.

Partner Brands

We partnered with four leading hospitality brands across Southern Africa.

Nia The Light

Klein Nettoh

The Journey

Three destinations across Southern Africa, each building on the narrative of the last.

Destination 01

Namibia

January 5 — January 8 · 3 nights

Namibia opened the campaign with cinematic landscape storytelling. Here Nia was joined by filmmaker Klein Nettoh, whose expertise in wildlife and desert cinematography allowed for premium, slow-paced visuals.

Story themes

  • Etosha National Park game drives
  • Onkolo Hide wildlife photography
  • Bush breakfast experiences
Destination 02

Johannesburg

January 8 — January 11 · 3 nights

Johannesburg introduced a contemporary African chapter focused on culture, art and modern city luxury. Klein Nettoh continued alongside Nia, bringing cinematic production value to urban luxury storytelling.

Story themes

  • Art, design and modern culture
  • Urban luxury and local experiences
  • Contemporary food and lifestyle
Destination 03

Mozambique

January 11 — January 14 · 3 nights

Mozambique closed the campaign with warmth, softness and ocean-led storytelling. Klein Nettoh continued alongside Nia for this final chapter, capturing coastal landscapes, island rhythm and sun-driven visuals.

Story themes

  • Ocean and island life
  • Slow pace and sunset storytelling
  • Marine experiences
