The Destinations

Three island nations, each offering a unique perspective on Indian Ocean luxury.

Mauritius

Refined luxury and multicultural richness. World-class resorts set against pristine lagoons, offering a unique fusion of African, Indian, and European influences.

Story themes

  • Luxury resort experiences
  • Water sports and lagoon life
  • Cultural fusion and local cuisine

Seychelles

The campaign's most visually striking chapter. Iconic granite boulders, powder-white beaches, and crystal-clear waters that define tropical paradise.

Story themes

  • Iconic beach landscapes
  • Private island experiences
  • Marine conservation and snorkelling

Madagascar

Adventure and biodiversity. Endemic wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, luxury eco-lodges, and extraordinary natural encounters from lemurs to baobabs.

Story themes

  • Unique wildlife encounters
  • Baobab landscapes
  • Eco-luxury and conservation
