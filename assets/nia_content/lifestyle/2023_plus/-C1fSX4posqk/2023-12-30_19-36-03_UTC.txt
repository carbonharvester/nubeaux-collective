THANK YOU #2023. 🦋✨💕 — It’s been a journey to say the least with an abundance of highs and lows. I grew in so many ways and I’m thankful for all the lessons I learned along the way. I thank you all for riding with me, supporting my collaborations, visiting my salon and being a great community. I look forward to what 2024 will bring. In true Nia tradition, see my top lessons from the year below.. 

1. You set the standard for how people treat you and see you. It all starts with how you treat yourself. If you want to be treated with respect then be sure to respect yourself. 

2. If you want to be forgiven, learn to forgive. No-one is perfect. 

3. Even in the midst of chaos, eat and look after yourself. You cannot be at your best if your cup is empty. 

4. Healing is not always going to look and feel pretty. It requires uncomfortable conversations, clearing out the hurt deep within and sitting down with your younger self. Go at your own pace and give yourself grace. 

5. Be kind to yourself, you are not always going to get things right the first time or make everyone happy. Move with positive intention and try your best.

6. Listen to your body, it will tell you to avoid situations/ people or decisions before they occur. Don’t ignore that gut feeling within.
