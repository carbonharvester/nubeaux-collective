I visited Kingston to go to the Bob Marley museum. They didn’t let us take pictures inside but there was one room with a poster and flag from Zimbabwe when he performed at their Independence ceremony. It gave me the chills seeing something so close to home there. I really learned a lot and now I’m even more obsessed with his music so if anyone gets into my car for the next few months be prepared to hear “Stir It Up” and “Jammin” on loop.
