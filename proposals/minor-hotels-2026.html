Executive Summary

Women control luxury travel. They make the decisions, they research the options, and they book the trips. Yet most luxury hospitality content still centres the couple or family experience. Solo travel content is not just for solo travellers. It shows a woman as the protagonist of the story, and that inspires every type of trip she might book: solo, couples, girls' weekends, family holidays, group travel.

This proposal positions Anantara through the eyes of a woman travelling on her own terms. The campaign is led by Nia The Light and Klein Nettoh, creators whose combined audience of 690,000+ represents exactly the decision-maker Minor Hotels needs to reach. This is a premium content series that speaks to the woman who books, regardless of who she eventually travels with.

We also see this as the beginning of a longer relationship. As Nia's life evolves, so does the content: from solo travel now, to subtle couples content, to eventually family travel. A creator who grows with the brand creates continuity, authenticity, and compounding audience familiarity.

2026 Milestone

A Double Anniversary

Anantara celebrates 25 years in 2026. The Royal Livingstone, one of the brand's flagship African properties, also turns 25. This is a rare alignment: a brand milestone and a property milestone in the same year, at a destination that defines African luxury.

Content created during an anniversary year carries additional weight. It becomes part of the brand's permanent archive, referenced in retrospectives and anniversary materials for years to come. This campaign delivers that legacy content.

The Solo Traveller Is Here

Solo travel is no longer a niche. Forbes, summarising Virtuoso trend data, reports that 71% of solo travellers are women. This is not a lifestyle choice made by a small segment. It is a dominant pattern in how luxury travel is consumed.

Virtuoso's Luxe Report identifies solo travel and experience-led luxury as defining trends for the next decade. Properties that understand this are reducing single supplements and creating solo-friendly inventory.

The 50+ Market Is Massive

JourneyWoman's Ageless Adventuress study estimates the women 50+ travel market will grow from $245 billion in 2025 to $519 billion by 2035 in the US and Canada alone. This is the primary growth engine of luxury travel.

Nia's substance, her entrepreneurial background, and her thoughtful approach resonate across age brackets. Women 50+ engage with aspirational content from creators who treat travel with the seriousness it deserves.

Diaspora Heritage Travel

The African diaspora seeking connection with ancestry, culture, and identity represents one of the fastest-growing segments in luxury travel. Heritage travellers have grown significantly since 2020 and show no signs of slowing.

Nia is half Zimbabwean and speaks Shona. Her heritage content connects authentically with this audience. She is not performing African identity — she is expressing it. That distinction matters to travellers seeking genuine cultural connection.

Audience and Buyer Logic

Understanding who makes the decision, and who needs to see themselves in the story.

Women Drive the Decision

Even when women travel with partners or families, they are typically the ones researching, shortlisting, and booking. Content that centres her experience speaks to every booking she will ever make.

Solo travel content inspires all travel. A woman watching Nia at Victoria Falls does not just imagine herself alone. She imagines bringing her partner, her mother, her girlfriends. She sees the destination through the eyes of someone like her, and that opens every door: family trips, girls' weekends, group travel, anniversary escapes. This content works because it puts her at the centre of the story.

Anantara's existing content library is heavily focused on couples. This campaign complements that library rather than duplicating it, filling a gap and expanding the visual language available to the brand.

A Woman of Colour's Perspective Is Additive

The diaspora traveller, the heritage seeker, the woman of colour considering a solo, friends, or family trip to Africa: she needs to see someone like her in the story. Not as a checkbox, but as a natural presence. Representation removes invisible barriers and signals that the experience is designed with her in mind.

There is a clear opportunity here. In 2025, just one Black woman appeared in Reels on both the Royal Livingstone and Stanley & Livingstone Instagram pages. Anantara Kafue River Camp, launching soon, currently has only eight posts. This campaign would meaningfully diversify the visual library across all three properties.

Nia brings this perspective authentically. Her Zimbabwean heritage, her Shona language content, and her audience of women who actively engage with her African travel posts make this alignment strategic, not performative.
